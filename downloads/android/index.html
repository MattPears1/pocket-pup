Pocket Pup Android Live Wallpaper

This APK installs a native Android live wallpaper that animates the dog sprite frames on a light background.

Download

Download Android APK

Android Steps

Download the APK on your Android phone. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app. Open Pocket Pup, tap Set live wallpaper, then choose whether to apply it to the Home Screen or both Home and Lock Screen if your phone supports that.
